# Geotechnical report (excerpt) – BW 12 Mühlbach (FICTITIOUS)

[[SEITE 13]]
## 3 Ground conditions, general
Four core drillings (BK 1 to BK 4) were sunk in the area of the replacement bridge. Below thin made ground (up to approx. 0.5 m) lie Quaternary river deposits of the Mühlbach.
The subsoil at the abutments is predominantly non-cohesive (sand, gravel). The density increases with depth.
BK 1 and BK 2 are located at Abutment 1 (west), BK 3 and BK 4 at Abutment 2 (east).
This excerpt is invented and serves demonstration purposes only.

[[SEITE 14]]
## 4 Abutment 1 and Abutment 2
**Abutment 1, boreholes BK 1 and BK 2:** sand and gravel, medium dense to dense, to at least 9.0 m below ground level; rock not explored.
For the bored piles (diameter 0.9 m, length 12 m) normal drilling progress in non-cohesive soil is to be expected at Abutment 1.
**Abutment 2, boreholes BK 3 and BK 4:** sand and gravel, medium dense, to approx. 8.0 m below ground level; stiff silt below.
Note: deviations of the layer boundaries between the boreholes cannot be ruled out.

[[SEITE 15]]
## 5 Groundwater
Groundwater was encountered in all four boreholes between 3.2 m and 3.6 m below ground level (status of investigation, fictitious).
Open dewatering of the excavation pits is to be provided for the construction period. The water level in the Mühlbach varies with the seasons.
